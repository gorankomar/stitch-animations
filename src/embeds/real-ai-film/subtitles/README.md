## Subtitle revisions — 2026-11-17

The two closing lines at 76.62–81.94 seconds (“Anyone can be a hero with Stitch AI” and “Real AI for real banking”) are intentionally empty in English because they are already shown in the video. They remain translated in Arabic, Vietnamese and Indonesian. Empty strings explicitly omit a cue for that language; the builder filters them from VTT and the English transcript. Raw recognition files remain unchanged as provenance.

Seven song cues cover the detected hip-hop vocals from 37.04–54.50 seconds, marked with ♪ in all four languages. The initial full-film transcription skipped this section. A dedicated local pass over 34–58 seconds with `no_speech_threshold=None` and the “Song lyrics.” prompt recovered the lyrics; its raw word timestamps are in `transcription.raw.song.en.json`. These lyrics and their translations remain drafts for listening/native-speaker review.
