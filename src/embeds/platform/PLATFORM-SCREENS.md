## Tool integration notes

- `set_component_instance_prop_values` uses `{prop_id, type:"string", string_value:"..."}` for both textContent overrides and the variant-id override. Include `scope_component_id` for nested instances in definitions.
- A ComponentSlot can be created with `element_schema:{type:"ComponentSlot",name:"Icon"}`; also call `set_display_name` on the returned Slot to set its visible/resolvable name. - WHTML builder processes at most five actions per call; batch accordingly and inspect every result.
